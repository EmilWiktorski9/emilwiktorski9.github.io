Which ceramic packages are available?

2, 3 and 5 year ceramic coating packages. Preparation, coverage and aftercare are confirmed with you before work starts.

What is Invisible Wiper glass coating?

A rain-repellent coating for glass. Water beads up and runs off, which helps visibility and makes the glass easier to clean.

Do you offer mobile detailing?

Yes, for boats and aircraft. Message the studio with the location and the vessel or aircraft.
